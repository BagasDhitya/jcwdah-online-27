Easy Homemade Fried Rice

A quick and delicious dish for a weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

2 cups of cold cooked rice, 2 cloves of garlic, and 1 tablespoon of soy sauce.

Cooking Steps

Step 1: Preparation

Chop the garlic finely and heat the oil in a pan.

Step 2: Cooking

Safety Tip

Be careful when adding rice to hot oil to avoid splashing.

Chef's Secret Note

Using day-old rice prevents it from getting mushy.
